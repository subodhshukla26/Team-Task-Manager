# 🎥 Team Task Manager - Demo Video Script

**Video Duration:** ~2 Minutes
**Focus:** Clean UI, User Flow, and Core Functionality.

---

## 🏁 Scene 1: Introduction (0:00 - 0:15)
**Visual:** Show the landing/login page with the beautiful 3D background or clean minimal design.
**Voiceover:** "Welcome to Team Task Manager—a modern, full-stack solution designed to streamline your team's workflow. Built with Next.js, Node.js, and MongoDB, it's fast, secure, and ready for production."

---

## 🔐 Scene 2: Authentication (0:15 - 0:35)
**Visual:** Screen recording of someone signing up. Show the role-based logic (Admin vs. Member).
**Action:** Enter email and password, click 'Sign Up'. Then log in.
**Voiceover:** "Getting started is easy. Our secure authentication system uses JWT and HTTP-only cookies to keep your data safe. The first user to join becomes the Admin, while subsequent users join as Members."

---

## 📊 Scene 3: The Dashboard (0:35 - 0:55)
**Visual:** Show the main dashboard. Hover over the Stat Cards (Total Tasks, Active Projects).
**Action:** Scroll down to see the 'Recent Tasks' and 'Upcoming Deadlines' sections.
**Voiceover:** "Once you're in, the Dashboard gives you a high-level overview of your team's productivity. Track active projects, monitor task statuses, and stay ahead of deadlines with our intuitive data visualization."

---

## 📁 Scene 4: Project & Task Management (0:55 - 1:30)
**Visual:** Navigate to the 'Projects' page. Click 'Create Project'.
**Action:** (Admin View) Fill in a project name and description. Then, navigate to 'Tasks' and create a new task. Assign it to a member and set a priority.
**Voiceover:** "Admins have full control. Create new projects in seconds and break them down into actionable tasks. Assign them to team members, set priorities, and watch the progress move from 'To-Do' to 'Completed' in real-time."

---

## 📱 Scene 5: Responsive Design & Closing (1:30 - 2:00)
**Visual:** Show the app resizing to a mobile view. Show the Sidebar collapsing.
**Action:** Click around the mobile menu.
**Voiceover:** "Whether you're at your desk or on the go, Team Task Manager is fully responsive, ensuring a premium experience on any device. Streamline your collaboration today."

**Final Visual:** Show the GitHub URL and the live deployment link.
**Voiceover:** "Check out the source code on GitHub and deploy your own instance on Railway. Thanks for watching!"

---

### 💡 Tips for Recording:
- **Use a Screen Recorder**: Use OBS Studio or Loom for high-quality capture.
- **Smooth Cursor**: Move the mouse deliberately and avoid quick, jerky movements.
- **Dark Mode**: If the app has a dark mode, show it off!
- **Transitions**: Use simple 'Fade' or 'Cross-Dissolve' transitions between scenes.
